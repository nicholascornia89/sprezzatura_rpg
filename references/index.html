    Refereferences & Inspirations

    The Glossary wishes to provide a quick reference and translation of the main elements of the game, using more common terminology shared by the TTRPG community. Furthermore, we are providing a list of sources of inspirations from academia, art, games and literature.

    Glossary

    Term TTRPG community translations
    Act A gaming session.
    Aspect Aspect, Trait
    Challenge Check
    Consonance System to collect succeses for players.
    Dissonance Disruptive force that players have to confront with.
    Game of the Goose Long term resource, like Stress, Conditions and HPs.
    Gathering Players at the table, including GM.
    Host The (temporary) Game Master.
    Major Arcana Mood or Theme of a Scene.
    Minor Arcana Metacurrency in form of cards, similar to Fate Points of Hero Points.
    Resonance Pool Dice bank for each player.
    Scene Encounter.
    Segment Tick of Clock.
    Temperament Attribute, main Stat.
    Triumph Success.
    Wheel Clock, Front.
